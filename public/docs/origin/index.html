The four rules

  1. 01

    Original art only

    Every mark on every garment is drawn by our own artists. If we cannot draw it, we do not sell it.

  2. 02

    Weight over hype

    240gsm minimum on tees, 480gsm on fleece. A drop is only limited if the garment is worth chasing.

  3. 03

    Made close to home

    Printed and packed in Naarm / Melbourne. Blanks are ethically sourced and we publish the mill.

  4. 04

    Say the real number

    Prices in AUD, tax in, no fake countdown discounts. Every measurement on this site is off the actual pattern.
