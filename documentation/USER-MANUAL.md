# ZENJI — User Manual

Everything a shopper can do, page by page.

## The house list (`/account`) — sign up / sign in

- **Join the list:** pick a handle (3–16 chars a–z/0–9/dash), a name for your card, and a passcode (4+ chars).
- Your passcode is **salted + hashed** and stored only in your browser — this is a static concept
  build, there is no server and no email.
- Everything you earn travels with your handle: cred + rank, loadout, Fit-Lab prefs, saved closet
  looks, counter slips, arcade scores, omikuji.
- Signing in swaps your saved world in; signing out saves it back. The header chip shows `@handle`.
- Your **member card** shows name, handle, rank (Genji → Rōnin → Kage → Sensei → Ukiyo Legend),
  cred points and join date.

## Showroom, Drop, Lookbook (`/`, `/drop`, `/lookbook`)

Browse the catalogue; open a piece for prints, fabric notes, size chart and the fitting-room link.
`LOADOUT` (top right) is your bag; `SEARCH ⌘K` jumps anywhere.

## Fit Lab (`/fit-lab`)

Two measurements → a recommended size per garment cut (regular / oversized etc.). Your result is
remembered and used by the Closet and product pages.

## The Closet (`/closet`) — the fitting room

- **Work the rail:** click pieces to hang them on the figure. Tees sit over pants, hoodies over
  tees, caps on top — like real clothing.
- Garments render as **worn silhouettes** sized from the live chart: an S and a 2XL genuinely
  sit differently. Change size or frame (F/M/U · athletic/classic/boxy) and watch the fit change.
- **Style Roulette:** one tap spins the rack and dresses the figure for you.
- **Outfit slots:** save up to three looks and recall them.
- Earning: hanging pieces and roulette pay street cred.

## The Counter (`/counter`) — haggle with KAGE

- KAGE puts three pieces under the counter **daily**. Pick one; he opens at tag price.
- Moves: **Flatter** (warms him, small cuts), **Bluff** (big cuts if believed, mood drops if not),
  **Cash** (steady; near the floor he signs), **Walk** (a gamble — he may call you back with the
  real price, or let you go).
- You get 4 moves (a 5th from Rōnin rank up). The bar shows tag → rumoured floor.
- **Shake on it** closes the deal: you get a counter slip (% off) + cred. Best cuts per piece are
  remembered. Slips are bragging rights in this concept build, not checkout codes.

## Drop Day (`/drop-day`)

Live release console: countdown, queue position theatre, restock pings.

## Shrine (`/shrine`)

One omikuji fortune per day.

## Studio (`/studio`)

Design your member card (type, seal, colourway) and export it.

## The Arcade (`/arcade`)

- **KOMA** the street cat hosts; dress him in any print.
- **Slash the Drop:** timing blade, combos, high scores.
- **Versus:** crown the better look, head-to-head.
- **Tag Wall:** leave a tag on the studio wall.

## Concierge (the bell, bottom right)

Opt-in clerk **KIRA**: answer three questions, get a three-piece pull you can hang straight into
your loadout. She never speaks first and remembers your taste. She will also point you at the
Counter when you want a better price.

## Street cred

Small rewards for doing things (hanging pieces, haggling, games, exporting a card). Ranks unlock
flavour — like the Counter's fifth move.
